Midpoint Progess Summary - Wine Quality Dataset (Chosen Dataset)

Dataset and goal: I am using the Wine Quality dataset, which contains chemical properties
of various wines. The goal is to analyze and understand how the features relate to the quality 
of the wine. 

Data cleaning: No major issues were found. Only cleaing required was dropping the ID column (empty data)
and dropping the quality column (to prevent cheating the values). Addtionally, I confirmed there was no 
missing values. 

Current PCA: The PCA for the scaled features shows that the first nine chemical components explain 95% of
the variance. Added a cumulative explained variance plot shows how variance builds. Also a scatter plot of PC1
vs PC2 colored by wine quality where similar qualities are bunched together.
